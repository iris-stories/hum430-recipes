against the return of the gout [^ref1]  
2 oz of sulfur [^ref2]  
2 oz of \[N...\]  
2 oz of gum guaiacum  
2 oz of wheat bowl  
6 oz of treacle [^ref3]  
Make it into a paste  
Take 2 tea spoonful  
Either in a bowl or  
Mixed in water

For \[...\] in \[...\]  
1 oz of \[...\]  
1 oz of balsam of sulfur  
1 oz of balsam of \[...\]  
All mixed up together  
Half a tea spoonful  
In half a \[...\] of \[...\]   
Going to \[...\]

[^ref1]: Gout is an old version of arthritis that plagued the rich in the 1700s. It was frequently known as the “disease of kings” because of its association with food and drinks that the rich consumed.  
[^ref2]: Sulfur (also spelled sulphur) was frequently used for skin treatments and medicine. It was thought to be connected to the human soul. Frequently used with mercury. Balsam of sulfur was made by boiling flowers of sulfur and used both topically and orally to treat various diseases.  
[^ref3]: Treacle was originally used for medicinal purposes and was mixed with sulfur to mask its bitter taste and to purify the blood of the children who took it.
