A Marrow Pudding
Make a Pint of Cream boyle[^ref1], then take it off
And slice in a peny[^ref2] white Loaf, when cold put in 8
Ounces of blanched Almonds, beat fine with 2 Spoon
Full of Orange Rose water[^ref3], put in the yolks of 6
Eggs a Glass of Sack[^ref4] a little Salt 6 Ounces of Candied
Lemmon[^ref5] (lemon) and Cittron[^ref6] sliced thin a pound of marrow
thin Sliced, mix all together, then put it into a 
Butter Dish, dust on fine Sugar and bake it
Serve it, add ½ a pound of Currants

##Footnotes
[^ref1]: Old spelling of boil.
[^ref2]: Old British term for a loaf of cheap bread, so named because it cost a penny a loaf. Also called a penny bun.
[^ref3]: Rose water is a substance made by steeping rose petals in water, used for culinary dishes and cosmetics as well as religious practices in Europe and Asia.
[^ref4]: According to wikipedia, old term referring to a white “fortified” wine. The only commonly consumed “sack” these days is sherry, so it is frequently cited as a synonym to such.
[^ref5]: I could find no evidence of this being a common spelling of Lemon, so I believe this is a misspelling.
[^ref6]: Unlike most citrus fruits, Citron is an “original”. There is some evidence that most citrus plants were derived from a select few species that were cross-bred extensively, and citron is considered one of these parent species.
