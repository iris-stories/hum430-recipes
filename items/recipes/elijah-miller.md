Take the head of a turbit [^ref1] and wash it very clean cut it into pretty big pieces soak it in some salt and water when it boils put in your turbit to (scald?) then take it out to cool and drain then season it with pepper salt and (mace?)[^ref3] and (nutmeg?) if you - [^ref2] it sweet you must (currans?) a little wine and butter if you (prefer?)[^ref3] savory put in oysters, (cockles?) and (capors?)



[^ref1]: ![alt text](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/37/Turbit.jpg/500px-Turbit.jpg)

[^ref2]: The word prefer is likely missing here given the context

[^ref3]: Spellings are highly phonetical throughout the manuscript (maece=mace, prefer=brefer)
