To Stew Pidgeons[^ref3]  
To a dozen of pigeons put a quart or 3 pints of water put into each of thayr bellys parsley and a pice of butter as big as a little walnut & a little pepper and salt when ye water do boyels put in ye pigeons and a bunch of sweet herbs if you please & when it hath stewed a little put in near a P0 of butter. Let it stew till they are ready. Put sum sypetts of white bread in your dish, and put ye broth and pigeons in ye dish. So serve it up.

To Bake Herrings [^ref3] 
Take fresh herrings and snip of (off) ye heads and ye tayles, and put ym in an earthen pan & lay a layer of ym & a layer of spice, cloves & mace, & a little salt, and so do till ye pot is full & fill up ye pot with vinegar & bake them in ye oven with household bread.

To Pickle Cowslip Flowers  
Take your cowslip flowers & pick ym out of ye husk & a layer of ym & a layer of white sugar into a pot till ye pot is full yn fill ye pot up with vinegar and let it stand for 3 or 4 dayes and yn thay are a good (salitt)??? for mutton.
